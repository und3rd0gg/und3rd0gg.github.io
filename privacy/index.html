Privacy policy

Updated 28 October 2026 · Publisher: underdogg

Notes and local storage

On Android, saved-text previews in the note browser widget are off by default. Enabling them exposes the displayed note text to your launcher and anyone viewing your home screen. The bounded derived preview cache is stored locally and excluded from Android backup. Turning previews off clears the cache and replaces widget text with a neutral message.

UnderdoggNote stores Ephemeral notes, local recovery information and settings on your device. Structured notes are ordinary UTF-8 Markdown files in the Obsidian vault and optional relative folder you select. The app does not add application-level encryption to these note files or its note databases.

On Android, filesystem-vault operations can request the system's broad shared-file access permission. That permission is wider than a single selected folder; the app uses it for the configured local vault and file operations. The operating-system permission screen shows the access being granted.

Deleting a note changes its active note state. Earlier content can remain in synchronization history, conflict copies, local archives, exported files or operating-system backups. Closing or uninstalling the app is not a command to erase all of those copies.

Microsoft Store licensing and purchases

The Microsoft Store version for Windows reads the app license status and trial expiry from Microsoft’s licensing service. These checks do not send your note text to Microsoft or connect your Google account. Purchases and payment details are handled by Microsoft Store; the app does not collect payment-card details. See Microsoft’s privacy statement.

Optional Google synchronization

Google synchronization is optional. With your authorization, the app uses your Google account identifier and email to connect the selected account. It requests openid, email and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. These OAuth scopes do not grant general access to your ordinary Google Drive files, contacts, Gmail or photographs.

The synchronized library contains Ephemeral note content and revision history, deletion markers, Account/Platform Settings, and device metadata such as installation identifiers, device names, platform and synchronization state. This information is stored in the application's private Google Drive appDataFolder and transferred between your connected installations.

Structured vault files, local filesystem paths, window geometry, the current note selection and launch-at-login preferences are outside this synchronization. Your operating system or a separately chosen vault-sync service can handle those files independently.

The app uses HTTPS for Google API requests. Synchronization does not provide end-to-end encryption: Google hosts the synchronized data, and authorized installations can read it. The implementation does not send note content to a maintainer-operated backend or use it for advertising or AI training.

Sign-in and credentials

Windows/macOS sign-in uses your system browser and the operating system's credential store. Android authorization uses Google Play Services; short-lived access tokens remain in the native part of the app, while a private subject/email binding lets the app request the selected account again. Tokens are not passed to the note WebView. Google and the operating-system providers have their own services and data practices.

Disconnecting Google stops this app's synchronization and clears its current authorization state. It retains local notes/recovery data and does not erase the cloud library or revoke the entire Google project grant. You can manage the grant through your Google account.

Delete cloud sync data removes content and portable settings from the retired synchronization generations after confirmation. Minimal reset/barrier metadata can remain to stop an old offline installation from silently uploading the retired history again. Local archives, exports and other installations' local copies require separate handling. Explicit rejoin can upload retained local text into the new library.

On Windows, the interface uses Microsoft Edge WebView2. The Microsoft runtime can collect diagnostic information and includes Microsoft Defender SmartScreen, which can send security-related information to Microsoft. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement and SmartScreen privacy information. These Microsoft components have separate terms and practices.

Other network requests and sharing

Currency calculations can download a fixed public USD rate table from cdn.jsdelivr.net or latest.currency-api.pages.dev. These requests do not include note text, expressions or a Google token. Like other network requests, they expose network information such as your IP address to the destination service.

When you choose export, move, Share or an external link, the selected file, application, storage provider or browser handles the requested destination. The app does not control the destination provider's retention or privacy practices.

The app has no automatic sync-telemetry upload. Its built-in synchronization diagnostic log records timestamps and event results. Diagnostic export saves a file to the destination you select; Android can use the document providers available on your device. The app does not send that file to the maintainer automatically. Review any file before choosing to share it.

Operating-system backup and restoration

Android app-data backup or device transfer can retain notes and settings according to your operating-system configuration. The backup rules exclude credential ciphertext, device-specific Google account bindings and the restoration marker. An external Obsidian vault is outside this app-data backup. Restoration assigns a new synchronization installation identity while retaining the restored notes.
