The ideas behind UnderdoggNote

UnderdoggNote is an independent project built on ideas from apps I admire. Note modes triggered by keywords draw on Antinote; the math is inspired by Numi and Soulver.

Thanks to their creators.

Questions and answers

How much does it cost?

UnderdoggNote is free. You can sync through your Google Drive without a separate app subscription.

What can the app see in Google Drive?

Only data it creates in its hidden Drive app-data folder; it doesn't request access to your other files. Sync is not end-to-end encrypted. See the privacy policy.

Does the app collect data?

The app does not automatically send note contents or diagnostic logs to the developer. The privacy policy explains the connected services and their data handling.

Does it work offline?

Local notes and ordinary calculations work offline. Sync and fresh exchange rates need an internet connection; cached rates can be out of date.

Can I change Alt+A?

Yes. You can change the show/hide shortcut in Settings.

Will there be an iPhone version?

There isn't one yet. If you'd like one, send me an email.

Found a bug or missing something?

Email me at und3rd099@gmail.com. I'd like to know what works for you and what doesn't.
