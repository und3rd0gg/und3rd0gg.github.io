Working with your notes

App notes or vault files

App keeps notes inside the application. Vault opens Markdown files in your selected Obsidian vault.

Copy or move a note

In App, open Export and choose Obsidian, .md or .txt. Copy keeps the App note; Move removes it after the file is saved and verified.

Sync App notes through your own Google Drive

Turn it on in Settings. App notes also stay on your device. Vault files use your vault’s own sync.

A separate folder for your notes. The rest of your Drive stays untouched.

No sign-up

For local notes, you don't need an app account or a Google sign-in. Install it and start typing.

Questions and answers

How much does it cost?

The 14-day free period runs from October 31 through November 13, 2026. From November 14, the Windows app will cost $5 in the US as a one-time purchase, with no subscription. Microsoft Store shows the price for your country. Sync through your Google Drive needs no separate app subscription.

What can the app see in Google Drive?

Only its own hidden folder. It can't see your other Drive files, your Gmail or your photos. Sync isn't end-to-end encrypted; the privacy policy has the details.

Does the app collect data?

The app doesn't automatically send me note text or logs. Google, Microsoft and the currency-rate service have their own rules; the privacy policy lists them.

Does it work offline?

Local notes and ordinary calculations work offline. Sync and fresh exchange rates need a connection; a saved rate, if available, may be out of date.

Can I change Alt+A?

Yes, in Settings.

Will there be an iPhone version?

Not yet. If you want one, email me. Your interest helps me decide what to build next.
