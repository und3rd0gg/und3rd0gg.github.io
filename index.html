01 / CAPTURE

Open. Write. Carry on.

A small window stays within reach. Capture a draft reply, a meeting thought or a short checklist without choosing a folder first.

02 / WORK IT OUT

Give the thought some room.

Use plain text, Markdown tasks and quick calculations. Local editing works without signing in or turning on Sync.

03 / KEEP WHAT MATTERS

Send it to Obsidian.

Choose your local vault, then export a note when it becomes worth keeping. Markdown and plain-text exports work too.
