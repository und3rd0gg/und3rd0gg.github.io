Your notes, within reach

Small window. Useful details.

Tray access on desktop. Quick capture and home-screen widgets on Android. The note browser can show saved text after you explicitly enable previews.

Know where your notes go.

Local editing needs no account. Optional Sync uses the app’s private Google Drive storage and is not end-to-end encrypted. No advertising or maintainer-operated note backend is included.
