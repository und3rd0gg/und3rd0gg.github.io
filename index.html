Questions and answers

How much does it cost?

The Windows app is free through November 11, 2026. From November 12, it will cost $5 in the US as a one-time purchase, with no subscription. Microsoft Store shows the price for your country. Sync through your Google Drive needs no separate app subscription.

What can the app see in Google Drive?

Only its own hidden folder. It can't see your other Drive files, your Gmail or your photos. Sync isn't end-to-end encrypted; the privacy policy has the details.

Does the app collect data?

The app doesn't automatically send me note text or logs. Google, Microsoft and the currency-rate service have their own rules; the privacy policy lists them.

Does it work offline?

Local notes and ordinary calculations work offline. Sync and fresh exchange rates need a connection; a saved rate, if available, may be out of date.

Can I change Alt+A?

Yes, in Settings.

Will there be an iPhone version?

Not yet. If you want one, email me. Your interest helps me decide what to build next.
