How can we help?

For app support and privacy questions, email und3rd099@gmail.com.

Include your app version, operating system and a short description of what happened. Please do not send passwords, Google authorization codes or private note content.

Keep your notes safe

Before changing installations or signing identities, export important notes. An Android QA installation and a permanent-key release use different signatures; updating requires the matching signature.

Google connection

Google Sync is currently in testing and access is limited to invited accounts. Local editing works without a Google account. You can manage an existing grant through your Google account.

Privacy requests

Use the same contact address for a privacy request. Cloud library removal, local files, exports and operating-system backups are separate copies; the app’s privacy policy explains those boundaries.
