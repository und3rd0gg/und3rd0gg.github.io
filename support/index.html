App, Vault and Export

По-русски

App saves notes inside UnderdoggNote. Vault works with Markdown files in the Obsidian vault selected in Settings.

Hover at the top edge for Catalog, New and the mode switch. The bottom edge reveals navigation and, in App mode, Export. Keyboard focus also reveals these controls.

Export offers Copy and Move to Obsidian, Markdown or plain text. Copy keeps the App note. Move removes it only after saving and verifying the destination. Canceling Save As keeps the note.

Google Drive sync covers App notes and supported preferences. Vault files need your own vault-sync setup. Exclusive fullscreen apps may cover the scratchpad.

Before you reinstall

Export the notes you care about before reinstalling or switching versions. Back up your Obsidian vault separately.

Google Drive sync

Sync is optional. App notes work locally without a Google account. Connect Google in Settings to sync them; Vault files are separate. You can revoke access in your Google account.

Privacy requests

Email the same address. Deleting cloud sync data leaves local files, exports and operating-system backups in place. The privacy policy has the details.
