Keep your notes safe

Before reinstalling or changing installations, export important notes from the app. Keep a separate backup of your Obsidian vault files.

Google connection

Connect your Google account in Settings and approve the requested access. Sync is optional. Local editing works without a Google account. You can manage an existing grant through your Google account.

Privacy requests

Use the same contact address for a privacy request. Cloud library removal, local files, exports and operating-system backups are separate copies; the app’s privacy policy explains those boundaries.
